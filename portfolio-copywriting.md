## Writing Style Guidelines

### Voice Characteristics
- **Confident but approachable** - Expert knowledge without ego
- **Results-oriented** - Focus on business outcomes and metrics
- **Client-centered** - Emphasize solving their problems
- **Clear and direct** - No jargon or fluff

### Key Principles
1. **Lead with value** - What's in it for the client?
2. **Show, don't tell** - Use specific numbers and examples
3. **Address pain points** - Acknowledge and solve real problems
4. **Build trust** - Be authentic and transparent
5. **Create urgency** - Limited availability or opportunity

### Avoid These Phrases
- "I think..." (Be confident)
- "Basically..." (Don't oversimplify)
- "Very/Really/Quite" (Weak modifiers)
- "I can..." (Show, don't tell)
- Generic buzzwords ("synergy," "paradigm")
